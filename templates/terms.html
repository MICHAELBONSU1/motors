3. Seller Posting Rules & Monthly Subscription Fee

Monthly Seller Posting Fee: GH₵ 50 / Month

To maintain the highest caliber of vehicle listings and prevent spam, registered customers pay an active subscription of GH₵ 50 per month to Larry (0549255121 via Mobile Money) before vehicle posting access is activated.

Every seller agrees to adhere strictly to the following listing obligations:

  • Legal Ownership & Authority: You must be the legal owner of the vehicle or possess certified power of attorney from the registered owner to sell it.
  • Accuracy of Specifications: All information provided—including make, model, year of manufacture, price (in GH₵), true odometer mileage, transmission type, fuel type, and condition (Brand New, Foreign Used, Local Used)—must be 100% accurate.
  • Genuine Vehicle Photographs: Photos uploaded must be genuine, recent, and depict the actual vehicle being offered for sale. Stock images of non-identical cars are prohibited.
  • Strict Prohibition on Stolen or Tampered Cars: Listing stolen vehicles, cars with altered VIN numbers, or vehicles with rolled-back odometers is illegal and will result in immediate permanent account termination and immediate handover of all recorded telemetry to the Ghana Police Service.

4. Buyer Protection & Mandatory Physical Inspection

While BIG-L Autos conducts initial listing reviews, buyers are reminded that automotive purchases represent significant financial transactions.

MANDATORY BUYER ADVISORY:
Never transfer full payment or deposits to any private seller before physically inspecting the vehicle, taking it for an operational test drive, and verifying the chassis number / registration papers with the Driver and Vehicle Licensing Authority (DVLA).

All negotiations and final purchase contracts between third-party sellers and buyers are conducted at their own discretion. For dealership-owned inventory, BIG-L Autos provides in-person viewings at our Somanya-Roundabout showroom.

5. "We Sell, We Buy, We Swap" Submissions

Customers utilizing our vehicle trade-in and cash buyout evaluation tool understand that initial estimates generated through chat or online submissions are non-binding.

A formal cash buyout or vehicle swap is concluded only after physical inspection of mechanical condition, exterior bodywork, tire wear, and DVLA documentation at our dealership location.
