2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to facilitate direct vehicle sales, buyer inquiries, and seller postings:

  • Account & Profile Information: Full name, verified email address, telephone / WhatsApp contact number, and encrypted passwords. For users authenticating via Google or Apple, we receive basic authentication tokens and verified profile emails.
  • Vehicle Listing Data: When an approved seller or admin posts a vehicle, we collect the vehicle make, model, year, asking price (in GH₵), mileage, transmission, fuel type, vehicle condition, exterior color, features, and photographic images.
  • Buyer Inquiries & Live Chat: Messages, pricing negotiation requests, and vehicle inquiries exchanged between prospective buyers and sellers/dealers.
  • Sell & Swap Submissions: Vehicle details submitted for cash buyouts or vehicle trade-in evaluations.
  • Technical Telemetry: IP addresses, browser types, and session timestamps used solely for security auditing and preventing fraudulent automated postings.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To display verified vehicle listings in the public showroom catalog.
  • To connect interested car buyers directly with dealers or authorized sellers via live real-time messaging or telephone.
  • To manage seller posting subscriptions (GH₵ 50 / month) and grant showroom publishing privileges.
  • To deliver critical transactional notifications, such as account confirmation, password resets, and buyer inquiry alerts.
  • To enforce showroom safety, detect fraudulent listings, and verify vehicle authenticity.

4. Mobile Money (MoMo) & Financial Security

Sellers wishing to post vehicles directly to the BIG-L Autos showroom pay a flat subscription of GH₵ 50 / month to Larry (0549255121 via MTN Mobile Money / Vodafone Cash / AirtelTigo Money).

Important Security Guarantee: All payment transactions occur directly on the telecommunication provider's secure network. BIG-L Autos never asks for, handles, or stores your Mobile Money PIN, banking passwords, or card numbers.

5. Data Protection, Storage & Encryption

We implement stringent technical security controls to safeguard your data:

  • Cryptographic Salting: Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using PBKDF2-SHA256. Raw passwords are never stored in plaintext.
  • Transport Security: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is secured using modern TLS/SSL encryption.
  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, lease, or distribute your personal contact information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

6. Your Rights Under Ghana Data Protection Act 2012

In accordance with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a full copy of the personal and vehicle data we hold on your account.
  • Rectification: Edit or correct any inaccurate personal details or car listing specifications directly through your customer dashboard or by contacting the admin.
  • Erasure: Request the complete deletion of your account, inquiry records, and vehicle listings.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Unsubscribe from marketing notifications or revoke seller posting permissions at any time.
